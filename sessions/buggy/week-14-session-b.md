# Week 14, Session B: Working with motors

**Route:** Buggy  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guides:** Use [Teach Computing’s robot buggy unit](https://teachcomputing.org/curriculum/key-stage-4/physical-computing-project) for the lesson goal and [Kitronik: using the motors](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-for-pico-motors) if using that kit. Follow your kit maker’s wiring and power directions.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../buggy/week-14-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Buggy kit, Pico, motor driver, two motors, its specified batteries, a stand that lets wheels spin freely.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Keep wheels off the floor for the first trials.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Have him write or change a short forward → stop → reverse sequence at low speed. Stop immediately if wiring heats or behaves oddly.
3. **Check (10 min):** Run, demonstrate or inspect the result. Ask what changed and what stayed the same.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Finished when

He demonstrates a reliable stop and can name the motor driver’s job. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Stop and disconnect power. Test one motor or sensor at a time using the kit maker’s instructions.
