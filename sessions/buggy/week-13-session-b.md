# Week 13, Session B: Inputs and outputs

**Route:** Buggy  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guides:** Use [Teach Computing’s robot buggy unit](https://teachcomputing.org/curriculum/key-stage-4/physical-computing-project) for the lesson goal and [Kitronik: buzzer, button and lights](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-pico-buzzer-button-lights) if using that kit. Follow your kit maker’s wiring and power directions.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../buggy/week-13-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Pico, buggy kit, its instructions, USB cable, button/LED test parts if needed, notebook.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Save the known working program and gather parts for a start/stop signal.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Add a visible start/stop signal or button rule. Show that the signal changes even with motors disconnected.
3. **Check (10 min):** Run, demonstrate or inspect the result. Ask what changed and what stayed the same.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Finished when

He identifies the input, the decision, and the output. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Stop and disconnect power. Test one motor or sensor at a time using the kit maker’s instructions.
