# Week 20, Session B: Improve and demonstrate

**Route:** Buggy  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guides:** Use [Teach Computing’s robot buggy unit](https://teachcomputing.org/curriculum/key-stage-4/physical-computing-project) for the lesson goal and [Kitronik: using the motors](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-for-pico-motors), [Kitronik: line-following sensors](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-pico-line-following) or [Kitronik: ultrasonic sensor](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-pico-ultrasonic-sensor) if using that kit. Follow your kit maker’s wiring and power directions.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../buggy/week-20-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Working buggy, saved code, same course, notebook.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Invite a family member if your child wants an audience.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Give a short demo. Let him explain the goal, one failure, the change he made, and what he would try next.
3. **Check (10 min):** Run, demonstrate or inspect the result. Ask what changed and what stayed the same.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Finished when

He can describe the path from sensor to decision to motor action. If doing both robots, start PiDog Session 13A next. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Stop and disconnect power. Test one motor or sensor at a time using the kit maker’s instructions.
