# Week 15, Session A: Build the chassis

**Route:** Buggy  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guides:** Use [Teach Computing’s robot buggy unit](https://teachcomputing.org/curriculum/key-stage-4/physical-computing-project) for the lesson goal and [Kitronik: using the motors](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-for-pico-motors) if using that kit. Follow your kit maker’s wiring and power directions.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../buggy/week-14-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Buggy kit, Pico, motors, wheels, batteries, screws, screwdriver, clear floor space.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Read the kit assembly guide and lay out screws and wheels in small containers.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Use Teach Computing Lesson 3 for the design goal. Assemble the chassis with him, then drive forward and stop on a clear floor. Then try one slow left turn and one slow right turn by running one wheel faster than the other.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “What could make the robot veer to one side?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and the assembled buggy drives forward, stops, and makes one slow turn each way. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Stop and disconnect power. Test one motor or sensor at a time using the kit maker’s instructions.
