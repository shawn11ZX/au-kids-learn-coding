# Week 14, Session A: Working with motors

**Route:** Buggy  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guides:** Use [Teach Computing’s robot buggy unit](https://teachcomputing.org/curriculum/key-stage-4/physical-computing-project) for the lesson goal and [Kitronik’s buggy tutorials](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-for-pico-motors) if using that kit. Follow your kit maker’s wiring and power directions.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../buggy/week-13-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Buggy kit, Pico, motor driver, two motors, its specified batteries, a stand that lets wheels spin freely.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Read the kit maker’s motor and power directions. Lift the board so the wheels spin freely, and check polarity with power off.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Use Teach Computing Lesson 2 as the concept guide. With adult help, run one motor at low speed, stop it, then run both.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “Which instruction stops the wheels?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and both motors can run and stop at low speed on a stand. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Stop and disconnect power. Test one motor or sensor at a time using the kit maker’s instructions.
