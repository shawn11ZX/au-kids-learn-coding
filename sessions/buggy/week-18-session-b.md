# Week 18, Session B: Finish one buggy behaviour

**Route:** Buggy  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guides:** Use [Teach Computing’s robot buggy unit](https://teachcomputing.org/curriculum/key-stage-4/physical-computing-project) for the lesson goal and [Kitronik: line-following sensors](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-pico-line-following) or [Kitronik: ultrasonic sensor](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-pico-ultrasonic-sensor) if using that kit. Follow your kit maker’s wiring and power directions.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../buggy/week-18-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Working buggy, tape path or cardboard obstacle, notebook.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Prepare the same test course for repeated trials.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Run three trials and improve one thing. If you plan to build PiDog after Week 20, complete the order check below.
3. **Check (10 min):** Run, demonstrate or inspect the result. Ask what changed and what stayed the same.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Order now if PiDog follows the buggy

- [ ] Confirm the [PiDog **V2** order from SunFounder](https://www.sunfounder.com/products/sunfounder-pidog-robot-dog-kit-for-raspberry-pi) from Week 17, or place it now. Add a Raspberry Pi **5**, 32 GB or larger microSD card and official Raspberry Pi **27 W** USB-C power supply for desk setup from the Australian buying options below.
- [ ] Check whether you need a microSD reader and whether the exact V2 kit listing includes its battery and charging parts.

See the [shopping list](../../11_year_old_robotics_python_curriculum.md#shopping-list) for the version and power notes.

## Finished when

The buggy demonstrates one behaviour and he explains what he changed; any PiDog order for the combined route is placed. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Stop and disconnect power. Test one motor or sensor at a time using the kit maker’s instructions.
