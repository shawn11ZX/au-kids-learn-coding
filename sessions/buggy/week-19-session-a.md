# Week 19, Session A: Plan his buggy challenge

**Route:** Buggy  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guides:** Use [Teach Computing’s robot buggy unit](https://teachcomputing.org/curriculum/key-stage-4/physical-computing-project) for the lesson goal and [Kitronik’s buggy tutorials](https://kitronik.co.uk/blogs/resources/online-tutorial-autonomous-robotics-platform-for-pico-motors) if using that kit. Follow your kit maker’s wiring and power directions.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../buggy/week-18-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Working buggy, masking tape, cardboard, ruler, notebook.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Clear a floor area and offer two modest challenge ideas.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Let him choose a one-sentence goal, draw the route and decide which existing robot behaviour to reuse.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “How will we tell whether the robot succeeded?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and there is a clear goal and a drawing of the test course. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Stop and disconnect power. Test one motor or sensor at a time using the kit maker’s instructions.
