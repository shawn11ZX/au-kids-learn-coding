# Week 11, Session A: Sound machine

**Route:** Pico  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Lesson:** Open [Introduction to Raspberry Pi Pico](https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/collections/physical_computing) and choose “Sound machine”. Use its circuit diagram for every connection.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pico/week-10-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Pico setup, passive piezo, buttons or dial, resistors, breadboard and wires in the project diagram.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Open “Sound machine”. Confirm the sounder is passive rather than an active buzzer.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Follow the guide to control a sound with a button or dial. Keep the volume and duration comfortable.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “What input starts or changes the sound?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and a button or dial controls sound. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Unplug before changing wires. Compare every connection with the project diagram, then run the last working code.
