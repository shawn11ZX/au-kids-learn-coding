# Week 22, Session A: Improve and demonstrate

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — Python examples](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/python/play_with_python.html) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-21-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Working PiDog V2, saved routine, clear floor, notebook.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Review the routine and decide how to stop it safely. Prepare three identical trials.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Run three trials and record the most common problem. Change one line or threshold, then repeat.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “Did the change help in the repeated trials?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and three trials and one improvement are recorded. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
