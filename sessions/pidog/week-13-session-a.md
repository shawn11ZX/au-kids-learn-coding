# Week 13, Session A: Meet the Raspberry Pi 5

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — PiDog setup guide](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/python/python_start/quick_guide_on_python.html) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete [Pico Week 12 Session B](../pico/week-12-session-b.md). If taking the buggy route first, complete [buggy Week 20 Session B](../buggy/week-20-session-b.md) instead.
- [ ] Have ready: PiDog V2 kit, Raspberry Pi 5, 32 GB or larger microSD, official 27 W USB-C desk power supply, computer and microSD reader.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Confirm the kit is V2. Follow SunFounder’s matching Pi 5 and Raspberry Pi OS setup instructions; prepare the microSD card.
- [ ] The PiDog **Standard** kit does not support Raspberry Pi 5. Use **PiDog V2** and its matching power/setup instructions.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Start the Pi 5 on the desk and open a terminal. Run `python3 -c "print('Hello, robot!')"` to check Python. In a text editor, save `print("Hello, robot!")` as `hello_robot.py`, then run `python3 hello_robot.py` in the terminal.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “How is this computer different from the Pico?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and the Pi 5 starts and runs a saved Python script. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
