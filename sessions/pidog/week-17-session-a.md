# Week 17, Session A: Finish assembly

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — PiDog calibration](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/python/py_0_calibrate.html) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-16-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: PiDog V2 parts, Pi 5, printed guide, screwdriver, clear desk and floor area.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Check cable routing and the kit battery/charging instructions before applying power.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Continue the remaining legs, head, tail and sensors in printed-guide order. Stop at the end of a guide step when time is up.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “Which part should we inspect before switching the robot on?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and the next guide steps are complete and cables are clear of moving joints. Finish any remaining steps before calibration in Session B. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
