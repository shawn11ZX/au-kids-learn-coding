# Week 15, Session A: Zero the servos

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — servo zeroing](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/python/python_start/py_servo_adjust.html) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-14-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: PiDog V2, Pi 5, power setup specified by SunFounder, labelled servos, printed instructions.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Read SunFounder’s V2 servo zeroing and power steps in full. Keep servo arms unattached until instructed.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** With adult handling power connections, follow the guide to zero one servo, then attach its arm exactly as directed.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “Why do we set zero before fixing the arm?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and the first servo is zeroed and its arm attached as the V2 guide directs. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
