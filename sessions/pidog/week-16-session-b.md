# Week 16, Session B: Build body and first legs

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — PiDog V2 documentation](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-16-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: PiDog V2 parts, printed guide, screwdriver, small containers, Pi 5 and kit power setup.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Check the first legs and cable routes before adding more parts.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Continue the legs in printed-guide order. Zero each servo as the guide directs before fixing its arm.
3. **Check (10 min):** Compare the assembled legs and cable paths with the next picture in the V2 guide. Keep power off while adjusting parts.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Finished when

The built joints match the guide and cables are free of moving parts. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
