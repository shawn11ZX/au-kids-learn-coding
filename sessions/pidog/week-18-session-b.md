# Week 18, Session B: First movement

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — PiDog V2 documentation](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-18-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Assembled and calibrated PiDog V2, charged kit power system, clear floor.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Keep two short working examples ready.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Run two supplied motions. Write down the visible result of each Python function and practice stopping the robot.
3. **Check (10 min):** Run, demonstrate or inspect the result. Ask what changed and what stayed the same.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Finished when

He safely starts and stops the robot and can name two actions. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
