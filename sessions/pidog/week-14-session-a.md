# Week 14, Session A: Understand joints and sort parts

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — PiDog V2 documentation](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-13-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: PiDog V2 kit, Pi 5, printed kit guide, paper, pencil, small screw containers.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Read the V2 parts list and keep parts separated by assembly step.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Draw a leg with two joints; use paper pieces to show how angles change its shape. Estimate one joint angle in degrees, for example 90° or 45°. Label the matching kit parts.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “What might happen if a servo arm is attached at the wrong starting angle?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and the parts are sorted and a two-joint leg is sketched. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
