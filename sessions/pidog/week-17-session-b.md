# Week 17, Session B: Finish and calibrate PiDog

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — PiDog calibration](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/python/py_0_calibrate.html) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-17-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: PiDog V2 parts, Pi 5, printed guide, screwdriver, clear desk and floor area.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Finish any assembly steps left from Session A first. Then open SunFounder’s complete-dog calibration guide.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Calibrate the assembled robot with adult supervision, record any offsets, and stop if a joint pushes against its limit.
3. **Check (10 min):** Use only the calibration check in the V2 guide. Record any offset and stop if a joint binds.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Finished when

The robot completes the maker’s calibration check without a trapped cable or binding joint. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
