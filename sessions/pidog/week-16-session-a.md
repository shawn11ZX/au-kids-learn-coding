# Week 16, Session A: Build body and first legs

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — PiDog V2 documentation](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-15-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: PiDog V2 parts, printed guide, screwdriver, small containers, Pi 5 and kit power setup.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Read the next V2 assembly pages and group screws by step.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Build the body and first leg assemblies with him. Let him identify parts and tighten accessible screws.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “Which way should this joint move, and is a cable in its path?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and the body and first legs match the printed guide. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
