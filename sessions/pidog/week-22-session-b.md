# Week 22, Session B: Improve and demonstrate

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — Python examples](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/python/play_with_python.html) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-22-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Working PiDog V2, saved routine, clear floor, notebook.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Invite a family member if your child wants to demonstrate it.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Show the final routine. Let him point to code he wrote and describe one improvement he would try later. Save the code.
3. **Check (10 min):** Run, demonstrate or inspect the result. Ask what changed and what stayed the same.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Finished when

He can safely start/stop PiDog and explain one sensor-triggered behaviour he made. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
