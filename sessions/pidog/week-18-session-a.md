# Week 18, Session A: First movement

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — PiDog V2 documentation](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-17-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Assembled and calibrated PiDog V2, charged kit power system, clear floor.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Review SunFounder’s stop and power-off method. Clear the floor and run the maker’s test first.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Run one supplied motion in an open area. Keep hands clear of moving joints.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “What should we do first if the dog moves unexpectedly?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and one supplied motion runs and the robot can be stopped safely. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
