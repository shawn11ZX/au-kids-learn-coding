# Week 15, Session B: Zero the servos

**Route:** PiDog  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Guide:** Open [SunFounder — servo zeroing](https://docs.sunfounder.com/projects/pidog/en/latest/python/python_start/py_servo_adjust.html) and the printed PiDog V2 guide. Follow the printed V2 guide for assembly and power.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../pidog/week-15-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: PiDog V2, Pi 5, power setup specified by SunFounder, labelled servos, printed instructions.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Unplug power before moving connections; lay out the next servos and screws.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Continue the printed assembly, zeroing each servo immediately before attaching its arm. Stop at a sensible checkpoint if time runs out.
3. **Check (10 min):** Compare each attached servo arm with the printed zero-position picture. Power off before inspecting its placement.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Finished when

The installed servo arms are aligned as the guide shows; nothing binds during the instructed test. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Pause at the last successful step in the V2 guide. For moving parts, stop and power off before inspecting them.
