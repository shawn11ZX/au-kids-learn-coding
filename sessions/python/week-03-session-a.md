# Week 3, Session A: Rocket launch

**Route:** Python  
**Time:** About 45 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Lesson:** Open [Rocket launch](https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/projects/rocket-launch) (part of Introduction to Python).

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../python/week-02-session-b.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Computer, browser, notebook.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Open “Rocket launch” and identify the loop in the example.

## During the session

1. **Warm up (5 min):** Ask what your child remembers from the last session.
2. **Work together (30 min):** Follow the project and watch the repeated animation. Change the starting fuel or countdown once.
3. **Check understanding (10 min):** Ask: “Which line repeats, and what makes it stop?” Let him point to the code, drawing or hardware when answering.

## Finished when

He answers the check question in his own words, and a loop runs a visible countdown or animation. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Read the first error line together, undo the last change, and rerun the last working version.
