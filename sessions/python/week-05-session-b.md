# Week 5, Session B: Don’t collide!

**Route:** Python  
**Time:** About 60 minutes. You may split it across days.  
**Lesson:** Open [Introduction to Python](https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/collections/python) and choose “Don’t collide!”.

## Before starting

- [ ] Complete the [previous session](../python/week-05-session-a.md), or repeat it if the checkpoint was difficult.
- [ ] Have ready: Computer, browser, notebook.
- [ ] Parent preparation: Open the saved project and clear a few minutes for three game trials.

## During the session

1. **Recall (5 min):** Look at what you made in Session A and ask what should happen next.
2. **Build or change (40 min):** Choose one change to the obstacle speed or collision rule. Play three times before and after the change and note what happened.
3. **Check (10 min):** Run, demonstrate or inspect the result. Ask what changed and what stayed the same.
4. **Record (5 min):** Write or draw the result and one idea for next time.

## Finished when

He describes the rule he changed and whether the game became easier or harder. If this takes longer, stop at a working point and continue next time.

If stuck: Read the first error line together, undo the last change, and rerun the last working version.
